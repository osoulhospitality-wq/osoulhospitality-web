Procurement and contract decisions

A shared record of prices and contracts

Each recommendation records its source, date, unit, assumptions and confidence level. Data, documents and analysis can be reviewed separately.

01 — SUPPLIERS

Supplier assessment

Review qualifications, licenses, performance, coverage, payment terms and trading history.

02 — PRICE

Products and pricing

Compare like for like across pack sizes, concentrations, delivery charges and taxes.

03 — CONTRACTS

Contract review

Identify scope, term, payment, renewals, service levels, exclusions and risks.

04 — GOVERNANCE

Approvals

Assign permissions, separate duties and keep an audit trail for the person reviewing a commitment.

05 — SAVINGS

Savings

Distinguish the opportunity identified, the amount negotiated and the savings verified on invoices.

06 — RISK

Risk review

Document financial, operational, legal and safety impacts with an owner and a closure date.
