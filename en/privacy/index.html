Privacy notice

Version 3.0 — last updated 22 October 2026.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

Osoul Hospitality determines the purposes for which information submitted through this website is processed. We are based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. For enquiries or to exercise your rights, email info@osoulhospitality.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line.

2. Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry, we may collect your name, organisation, business email address or phone number, property type, city, project stage and size, target date, the issue you describe, the support requested and whether you can share documents. Hosting logs may record your IP address, browser type and request time for security and operational purposes.

3. Purposes and grounds for processing

We use your information to respond, classify your enquiry, propose next steps for assessment, document communications, protect the form from misuse and meet applicable legal obligations. Depending on the circumstances, processing is based on your consent on submission, steps you ask us to take before entering a contract, a permitted legitimate interest that does not override your rights, or a legal obligation.

4. How the form works

Our hosting environment processes your enquiry. A restricted Supabase database stores the enquiry record. Enquiries containing a business email address are sent to HubSpot for follow-up. The system also attempts to send a copy to our designated business mailbox. The stored record enables recovery if a delivery channel fails. Confirmation appears when at least one delivery channel succeeds or the enquiry is saved for operational recovery.

5. Who receives your information?

Access is limited to the people responsible for assessing your enquiry and the providers needed to deliver the service. Hostinger provides hosting and email, Supabase provides restricted storage, HubSpot manages enquiries, and Google Analytics 4 measures visits after consent. We do not sell your data or share it for independent advertising purposes. If you choose WhatsApp or your own email service, the provider’s policy applies.

6. Where processing takes place

Service providers may process enquiry information outside Saudi Arabia. Our Supabase project uses the Asia-Pacific region (Seoul), and HubSpot uses its European data region. With your consent, Google Analytics may process analytics data through its global infrastructure. We assess applicable transfer requirements and safeguards and limit data to what the purpose requires. Please avoid submitting sensitive information, confidential records or identity documents through the public form. After initial assessment, we agree a secure channel and a non-disclosure agreement where needed.

7. Retention

We review non-contracted enquiries and delete or anonymise their data within 24 months of the last contact. You may request earlier deletion. A legal requirement or an active claim may require longer retention. Once a contract is in place, project documents follow the agreed retention schedule and applicable legal obligations.

8. Security

We limit collection, validate inputs, deter automated submissions, restrict mailbox access and apply security updates and backups appropriate to the hosting environment. Digital channels carry risk. Please use the public form only for non-sensitive information.

9. Your data rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to be informed about processing, access your data, obtain a copy in a suitable format, correct or complete your data, or request its destruction. Where consent is the basis for processing, you may withdraw it. You may also lodge a complaint. We verify identity and respond within the applicable period. The implementing regulations provide a timeframe of up to 30 days, with a lawful extension where necessary.

10. Cookies and analytics

Essential technologies deliver the pages and protect the enquiry form. Google Analytics 4 and HubSpot tracking load only with your explicit consent. Select “Essential only” or reopen “Privacy settings” at any time. Declining optional analytics does not affect the contact form or core site functions. We do not use advertising pixels or targeted-advertising cookies.

11. Complaints and updates

Email info@osoulhospitality.com first with a short description of your request. You may also refer to SDAIA’s data protection portal. We keep a record of policy changes. The version number and date appear at the top of this notice. External links and subsequent secure workspaces have their own terms and controls.
