Service enquiry privacy notice

The controller identified in the company details uses enquiry information to respond, scope work and manage the contractual relationship. Include only the property information needed to deal with the request.

Data and enquiry handling

We use your name, contact channel, requested service and any details you choose to add. Enquiries are accessible to the contracts team and technical providers as needed. The processing statement identifies recipient categories, processing locations and the legal basis for each purpose.

Retention and rights

You may request access, a copy, correction or destruction of your data under the applicable provisions. Send requests to the privacy contact below. You may also raise a complaint with the competent authority.

Processing details and rights requests

  • Email info@osoulhospitality.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line.
  • The site notice dated 22 October 2026 provides for review and deletion or de-identification of uncontracted enquiries within 24 months of last contact unless continued retention is justified. Contract records follow the agreed retention schedule and applicable obligations.
  • Analytics are optional and consent can be withdrawn in privacy settings. The contracts team uses enquiry details to respond and handle pre-contract discussions. [Approved lawful basis per purpose]
  • Hostinger provides hosting and mail, Supabase stores enquiries in Seoul, HubSpot manages enquiries in its EU region and Google Analytics 4 provides optional analytics. Processing locations follow the site notice dated 22 October 2026. [Approved transfer safeguards]
