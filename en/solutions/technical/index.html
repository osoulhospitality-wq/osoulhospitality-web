Technical maintenance and building systems

A lift outage or loss of cooling changes how guests use the building. We prioritise the impact, assign diagnosis to a qualified specialist and follow restoration through testing.

Request a service proposal

Technical coverage

The site plan identifies covered equipment, manufacturer instructions, access windows and work requiring a specialist contractor.

What you receive

The visit report identifies the asset, symptom, diagnosis, intervention and return-to-service check. A temporary repair retains a named owner for the permanent work.

Services available by agreement

Air-conditioning maintenance

Manage servicing and faults in air conditioning and ventilation systems to maintain guest comfort and keep facilities running.

Electrical and backup power

Oversee switchboards, electrical distribution and scheduled checks for generators and backup power.

Pumps, plumbing and water networks

Coordinate investigation of leaks, water supply, drainage and pump faults, followed by a specialist return-to-service test.

Lift and escalator maintenance

Coordinate routine servicing and breakdown response with qualified providers, and track outstanding findings.

Fire and life safety systems

Track inspections of detection and suppression systems, document findings and follow up with qualified providers.

Networks and communications

Coordinate on-site network and communications maintenance and track outages affecting guests or staff.

Resident maintenance technician

Arrange on-site technician coverage for daily checks, service requests and scheduled work.

On-demand field maintenance

Triage service calls, arrange a technician visit and document the work before sign-off.
