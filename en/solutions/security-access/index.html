Security guarding and access control

Coordinate security coverage and access procedures, and track incidents and findings with the contracted provider.

How we deliver the service

We agree posts and visitor and supplier entry procedures with the operator and guarding provider. Open incidents pass between shifts through the property’s escalation process.

What the owner receives

A post plan and access-controlled incident record showing actions taken.

How we verify quality

Management samples shift handovers and exceptions against the access-point coverage plan. Restricted records identify who resolved each gap and when coverage resumed.
