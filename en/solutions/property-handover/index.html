Opening readiness and unit handover

Review site readiness before opening or handover, list outstanding items and track closure with the responsible parties.

How we deliver the service

We inspect each unit with the receiving team, distinguish occupancy blockers from work that can be scheduled and recheck corrections before sign-off.

What the owner receives

A unit-level snag list and handover record identifying accepted items, exceptions and owners.

How we verify quality

Operational defects are reinspected before unit acceptance. The record distinguishes handover blockers from dated follow-up items so the operator knows which units can be released.
