Resident maintenance technician

Arrange on-site technician coverage for daily checks, service requests and scheduled work.

How we deliver the service

We agree coverage hours, inspection rounds and the technician’s scope. Open jobs carry over at shift handover and specialist work is referred to the appropriate contractor.

What the owner receives

A shift log showing completed work, pending parts or specialist visits, and who follows up.

How we verify quality

A shift handover records each outstanding request and its blocker. The supervisor can see what the resident technician resolved, what needs a specialist and what awaits parts approval, reducing duplicate dispatch.
