Asset, contract and cost management

Trace expenditure to an asset or service. Equipment, contracts and warranties establish who owns the repair decision, who can approve the cost and who accepts delivery.

Request a service proposal

Records that inform the budget

Fault history and purchase orders are tied to the asset. Contract inclusions are distinguished from additional spend. Repair and replacement options include the reasoning and limitations of the available evidence.

Services available by agreement

Integrated facilities management

Bring services, vendors and work orders into one operating plan with clear accountability for each action.

Contract and quality management

Review contract scope, visit schedules and service levels, and document findings and corrective actions.

Operating supplies and materials

Track demand for consumables and spare parts, coordinate supply and review usage.

Asset inventory and tagging

Build a register tying each asset to its identifier, location and key details so its history and servicing can be tracked.

Asset condition and lifecycle

Record asset condition and prioritize repair or replacement based on condition and the impact of failure.

Preventive maintenance planning

Set maintenance schedules by asset type and service interval, and track completed and overdue visits.

Service requests and corrective maintenance

Track each work order from triage and assignment through the visit report, verification and closure.

Technical handover and transfer of operations

Reconcile units and shared areas with the handover register and assign inherited defects to the party responsible for closure.

Civil Defense documentation

Organize safety-system records and coordinate outstanding documentation with competent authorities and licensed providers.

Hospitality property licensing support

Organize property documents and help the facility representative track licensing requirements and submissions.

Municipal licensing support

Organize the municipal licence file and follow up on findings and deadlines with the client and relevant parties.

Operating spend review

Review contract, callout and supply costs to identify where spending or service scope warrants adjustment.

Performance reporting and SLAs

Report work order status, visits, service quality and SLA performance, and flag where management decisions are needed.
