Asset inventory and tagging

Build a register tying each asset to its identifier, location and key details so its history and servicing can be tracked.

How we deliver the service

We survey by building, floor and room, record nameplate details and condition, attach a unique tag and test the scan at the asset before handover.

What the owner receives

A searchable register linking each tag to its location, photograph and equipment data, with missing fields flagged.
