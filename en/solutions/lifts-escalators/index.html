Lift and escalator maintenance

Coordinate routine servicing and breakdown response with qualified providers, and track outstanding findings.

How we deliver the service

We review stoppages by lift, coordinate attendance with the lift contractor and agree alternative guest access with reception. We track the specialist’s release before return to service.

What the owner receives

An equipment log showing downtime, visits, proposed parts and outstanding contractor findings.

How we verify quality

Each unit is ranked by downtime and recurring faults for the period. A contractor review uses the specialist’s return-to-service record and identifies planned maintenance separately.
