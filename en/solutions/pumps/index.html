Pump maintenance

Coordinate inspections and maintenance for water supply, drainage and pressure pumps based on asset condition.

How we deliver the service

We map each pump to its service area and trace low-pressure or drainage complaints. The specialist checks the pump, controls and duty rotation, then retests the affected area.

What the owner receives

An inspection record linking the symptom to the pump, test result and recommended action.
