Service requests and corrective maintenance

Track each work order from triage and assignment through the visit report, verification and closure.

How we deliver the service

We record guest or service impact, link the request to a location, asset and assignee, track delays and request a repair test before closure.

What the owner receives

A traceable work order from receipt to closure, including cause and evidence of restoration.

How we verify quality

We measure the outage from the documented interruption to restoration and record administrative closure separately. Recurring causes flag repairs that need another look.
