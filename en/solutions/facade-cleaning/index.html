Façade cleaning

Schedule façade and glazing cleaning, coordinate safe access and inspect the finished work.

How we deliver the service

We divide the façade by finish and access. The specialist defines the access method and exclusion area, and we schedule around busy entrances before inspecting finished sections.

What the owner receives

A section-by-section handover identifying completed areas and any inaccessible surfaces.

How we verify quality

The supervisor inspects completed sections from agreed viewpoints, recording residual marks and any rework. Payment applications can then be checked against the areas accepted.
