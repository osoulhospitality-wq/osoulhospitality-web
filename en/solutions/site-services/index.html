Housekeeping and site services

A residential corridor and an apartment awaiting check-in require different handovers. We zone the property by use and define service windows, inspection criteria and ownership of corrections.

Request a service proposal

What the supervisor checks

Supervisors check actual coverage, supplies, inspection findings and rework. The operator can distinguish areas ready for use from those awaiting cleaning or technical inspection.

Services available by agreement

Cleaning and housekeeping

Plan cleaning for guest units and shared areas, and check quality against site-specific standards.

Resident cleaning team

Arrange daily on-site staffing and shifts, and monitor attendance and service quality.

Façade cleaning

Schedule façade and glazing cleaning, coordinate safe access and inspect the finished work.

Tank cleaning and disinfection

Schedule specialist tank cleaning and disinfection, and keep records of work, findings and the next visit.

Pest control

Arrange preventive visits and callouts, record treated areas and follow up on recurring issues.

Waste collection and management

Organize collection points and pickup schedules, and follow up on delays and overflowing containers.

Security guarding and access control

Coordinate security coverage and access procedures, and track incidents and findings with the contracted provider.
