Operating supplies and materials

Track demand for consumables and spare parts, coordinate supply and review usage.

How we deliver the service

We review consumption, stock and lead times for critical items, compare equivalent specifications and quantities, then check deliveries against approved orders.

What the owner receives

A requirements list, comparable quotations and a record of quantity or specification discrepancies.

How we verify quality

The receiver checks quantity and specification against the purchase order. Consumption is then compared with occupancy or workload so the owner can investigate stock differences before reordering.
