Integrated facilities management

Bring services, vendors and work orders into one operating plan with clear accountability for each action.

How we deliver the service

We review contracts, assets and requests with the owner and bring their obligations into one operating plan. Overlapping work receives a named lead, decision and due date.

What the owner receives

One operating plan and an owner report identifying accountability and decisions due.

How we verify quality

The owner can trace a request from intake to acceptance, with the responsible party and reason for waiting at each handoff. This distinguishes a vendor delay from an internal approval delay.
