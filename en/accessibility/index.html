Accessibility

Using the website

Last updated: 8 August 2026.

Getting around

Use the skip link to jump to the main content. Headings, clear links, a logical focus order and visible focus states support keyboard navigation.

Language and reading direction

Arabic pages read from right to left and English pages from left to right. Use the language link to open the corresponding page.

Text and contrast

We review contrast, text size and responsive layouts across screen sizes. Interactive controls have a visible keyboard focus state.

Motion

Pages respect the reduced-motion preference in your device settings.

Images

Meaningful images have text alternatives. Decorative images are hidden from screen readers.

Enquiry form

The service enquiry uses one form with labelled fields and instructions before submission. Provide a phone number or choose email as your contact method.

Alternative formats

Some externally hosted documents may have accessibility limitations. Contact us if you need an alternative format for an Osool-created document.

Report a barrier

Email info@osoulhospitality.com with the page, the issue and the device or browser you used. We will assess the issue and prioritise it in our improvement cycle.
