Facilities management service terms

These terms apply when incorporated into a service proposal approved by the owner or its authorised representative and the identified service provider. The parties confirm written acceptance of scope and commencement date.

Contract documents and scope

The signed agreement, proposal and asset, pricing and service-level schedules form the contract. The order of precedence is set in the signed agreement. Site, covered areas, exclusions, coverage and deliverables are recorded before mobilisation.

Delivery and responsibilities

The provider coordinates personnel and delivery partners and checks in-scope work. The client provides authorised access and asset records and names an approver for work and cost. Each party promptly records material impediments, their impact and proposed action.

Fees and variations

The proposal specifies fees, materials, applicable taxes, invoicing and payment dates. Changes to scope or price require written approval from an authorised representative. Emergency spending limits and supporting evidence are agreed in advance.

Acceptance, correction and liability

Work is inspected against scheduled criteria. Objections record the reason, correction period and reinspection. The agreement sets workmanship warranties, liability and insurance consistent with applicable law. Unresolved items carry across shift handovers.

Confidentiality, term, termination and disputes

Site records are used for service delivery. Publishing the client’s name or images requires separate permission. The agreement specifies term, renewal, notice, cure, record handover and final settlement. Saudi law governs and the parties specify dispute resolution and the competent forum in their agreement.
