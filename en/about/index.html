About Osoul

Facilities and asset management for hospitality properties

We manage building systems and day-to-day services for hotels and serviced apartments, combining on-site oversight with the Osoul platform to track assets, work orders and supplier contracts.

Know who is responsible for each job

We maintain an asset register, a preventive maintenance plan, a service request log and a schedule of supplier visits. Reports show the condition of systems and services, overdue work and decisions awaiting your approval.

Technical systems

We follow up on HVAC, lifts, electrical systems, plumbing and fire protection.

Day-to-day services

We coordinate cleaning, security and waste management and check the service delivered.

Contracts and suppliers

We track scope, scheduled visits and the quality of completed work.

Who manages and checks the work

Our team coordinates daily facilities operations. When work requires statutory approval or a licensed signature, we involve the appropriate licensed specialist.

Project lead

Coordinates the operator and suppliers and raises decisions with the owner.

Technical specialist

Supports technical work and operations according to the property’s needs.

Quality reviewer

Reinspects corrected work and records the reason for acceptance or rejection against the work order.

Licensed specialist

Provides advice, approval or sign-off where a licence is required.

Owner reporting

Shows completed and overdue work and decisions that need approval.

From service request to closeout

We agree responsibilities at the start, assign an independent reviewer for the work and disclose potential conflicts before agreeing the scope.

How we follow up

  1. Record the request, asset and priority.
  2. Assign the work order to the relevant team or supplier.
  3. Monitor the visit schedule and the agreed service level.
  4. Inspect the result and document any findings.
  5. Close the order and update the asset register and report.

Independence and conflicts of interest

  • Disclose any relationship that could affect impartiality.
  • Separate recommendations from licensed approvals.
  • Disclose any supplier or operator commission.
  • Document exceptions and obtain the owner’s approval.

What we manage and what we refer

  • Manage facilities, assets, work orders, contracts and reporting.
  • Coordinate supplier visits, testing and handover with the operator.
  • Refer licensed engineering, legal and financial approvals.
  • Check the quality of work and verify closure of findings.

What you can review before appointing us

  • Professional history and available certification records for project leads.
  • A sample scope of work and responsibilities.
  • Anonymised sample reports, site visit records and quality checks.
  • Conflict of interest and confidentiality declarations.
  • Licensed specialist details when their work is in scope.

How we document work and responsibilities

  • Published results include supporting evidence, definitions and limits on their use.
  • We credit individual experience to the person who gained it.
  • We obtain written permission to use a government or client logo.
  • Licensed opinions are referred to qualified specialists.
  • We disclose conflicts of interest and verify each closeout against documented evidence.

Who is accountable at your property?

Facility management

The facility manager identifies which service is affected, agrees the priority with the operator and brings the owner any decision that needs approval.

Engineering and assets

The technical team reviews the asset history, test readings and warranty terms before recommending repair or replacement.

Contracts and quality

The contract owner checks the agreed service level. The quality reviewer examines the evidence and either accepts the work or returns it for correction.
