About Osool

Facilities and asset management for hospitality properties

We manage building systems and day-to-day services for hotels and serviced apartments, combining on-site oversight with the Osool platform to track assets, work orders and supplier contracts.

Technical facilities services

We coordinate preventive and corrective maintenance for HVAC, lifts, electrical, plumbing and fire protection systems.

Day-to-day facilities services

We coordinate cleaning, security and waste services and check the quality of delivery.

Assets and work orders

Our team records equipment, assigns work orders, tracks site visits and documents completed work.

Supplier and contract oversight

We track service schedules, contract obligations and supplier performance across the property.

Reporting for owners and operators

Our reports show completed work, unresolved issues and decisions that need your attention.

Development and opening support

We can also advise on hospitality development, handover and operational readiness.
