Renewing a Serviced-Apartment Licence Without a Last-Minute Rush

An annual calendar gives the operations team time to renew contracts, replace expired evidence and prepare the licence file.

By Y. A. — Osoul HospitalityUpdated 27 November 20261 min read

Worked example

An early renewal review flags a service contract that expires before submission. Assign its renewal to the contracts lead, then have the filing owner check the dates and name on the replacement.

What to check

  • Expiry dates of every linked document
  • Changes in ownership, management or capacity
  • Open findings from previous visits
  • Integrity and retrievability of approved copies

Steps to take

  1. Start the review well before the due date
  2. Refresh the document register monthly
  3. Close findings with photos and minutes
  4. Run an unannounced internal audit
  5. Put renewal ownership on management dashboards

What to measure

  • Valid-document ratio
  • Overdue findings
  • Resubmission requests

An evidence check for the owner

Flag documents due to expire before renewal. Distinguish evidence awaiting an authority’s decision from evidence the contractor has yet to deliver.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Relying on one employee's memory
  • Submitting an old document after an operator change
  • Deferring visible maintenance defects
