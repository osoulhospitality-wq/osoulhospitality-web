How to Build an Audit-Ready Compliance Register

Keep the source, owner, review date and evidence for each requirement in a single register.

By Y. A. — Osool Hospitality8 August 20261 min read

Worked example

Record a fire alarm test against the specific location, test date and report link. Keep the finding open if the report does not identify the zone that was tested.

What to check

  • Source and version of every obligation
  • Named executive owner
  • Next verification date
  • Evidence link and reviewer

Steps to take

  1. Give every obligation a unique ID
  2. Record the source without misleading truncation
  3. Support every status with dated evidence
  4. Add independent review for critical controls
  5. Preserve change history instead of overwriting it

What to measure

  • Evidence coverage
  • Average age of open findings
  • Reviews completed on time

Mistakes to avoid

  • A list with no evidence links
  • Vague accountability
  • Deleting the trail of earlier decisions
