Prioritising CapEx in a Hospitality Asset

Rank capital requests by safety, compliance, service continuity, equipment life and guest impact.

By Y. A. — Osoul HospitalityUpdated 27 November 20261 min read

Worked example

If one budget must cover a lobby refresh and repeated HVAC breakdowns, compare service disruption, cost of delay and expected return before ranking the investments.

What to check

  • Safety and compliance risk
  • Failure likelihood and impact
  • Revenue protected or generated
  • Remaining life and alternatives

Steps to take

  1. Score projects against common criteria
  2. Separate maintenance from enhancement
  3. Estimate the cost of inaction
  4. Sequence bundles and dependencies
  5. Review benefits after spend

What to measure

  • Risk reduced
  • Revenue protected
  • Cost and schedule variance

An evidence check for the owner

Compare repair and replacement using failure recurrence, downtime, spare availability and temporary cover. Present the cost of deferral separately from purchase price.

Mistakes to avoid

  • A wish list
  • ROI without a baseline
  • Deferring a critical asset for a cosmetic upgrade
