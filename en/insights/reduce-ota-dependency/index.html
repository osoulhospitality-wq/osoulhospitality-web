Reducing OTA Dependency Without Losing Demand

Build direct bookings and guest relationships while monitoring the demand brought in by online travel agencies.

By Y. A. — Osool Hospitality8 August 20261 min read

Worked example

Test a direct offer for returning guests who have opted in to contact. Compare its acquisition cost and net revenue with intermediary channels, and monitor total demand during the test.

What to check

  • Channel share and net contribution
  • Potential to convert repeat demand
  • Website and booking experience
  • Guest-data policy

Steps to take

  1. Separate new and returning demand
  2. Create direct-booking value
  3. Remove mobile and payment friction
  4. Activate post-stay communication
  5. Reallocate inventory gradually

What to measure

  • Direct share
  • Acquisition cost
  • Repeat-booking rate

Mistakes to avoid

  • Closing a channel overnight
  • Breaking parity without a plan
  • Collecting data that is never used
