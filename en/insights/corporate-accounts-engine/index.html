Corporate Accounts: From Company List to Demand Engine

Group corporate prospects by demand, agree on a follow-up schedule and track the room nights each account produces.

By Y. A. — Osool Hospitality8 August 20261 min read

Worked example

One account may produce steady midweek room nights while several signed contracts generate no bookings. Track realised nights, collections and renewal dates for each account.

What to check

  • Sectors and demand generators
  • Opportunity size and timing
  • Competition and contracting terms
  • Property capacity to serve

Steps to take

  1. Build a qualified account list
  2. Define a proposition for each segment
  3. Use a simple CRM
  4. Connect rate to volume and behaviour
  5. Review production and wash monthly

What to measure

  • Room nights booked
  • Opportunity conversion
  • Account productivity

Mistakes to avoid

  • Discounting without volume commitment
  • Measuring call volume
  • Keeping non-producing accounts
