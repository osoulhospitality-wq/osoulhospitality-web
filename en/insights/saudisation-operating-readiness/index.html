Saudisation as Operating Readiness, Not an Isolated Ratio

Connect the staffing plan to rosters, training and career paths so each shift can maintain service coverage.

By Y. A. — Osoul HospitalityUpdated 27 November 20261 min read

Worked example

If a hotel reaches its staffing target but still relies on one night supervisor, add a handover check and identify a trained backup for each critical duty.

What to check

  • Current roles, ratios and decisions
  • Gap by department and shift
  • Training and coaching capacity
  • Retention risks

Steps to take

  1. Record the governing source and review date
  2. Build a demand-based workforce plan
  3. Link recruitment to practical learning
  4. Name deputies for critical roles
  5. Measure retention, not hiring alone

What to measure

  • Compliance by category
  • Time to competence
  • Ninety-day retention

An evidence check for the owner

At shift change, trace each critical duty to a trained, authorised person and an available substitute. Record service interruptions attributable to gaps in cover.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Using a misleading total ratio
  • Hiring without a coach or roster
  • Renaming jobs without changing the work
