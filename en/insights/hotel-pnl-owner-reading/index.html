Performance & profitability

How Should an Owner Read a Hotel P&L?

Start with room rates, nights sold and booking mix, then trace how they affect the profit and loss statement.

By Y. A. — Osool Hospitality8 August 20261 min read

Worked example

If room revenue rises while margin falls, compare rate, volume and channel mix before looking at commissions and overtime. Check cash flow to see whether collections are keeping pace.

What to check

  • Budget and prior-year comparison
  • Departmental drivers
  • Non-recurring items
  • Allocations and management fees

Steps to take

  1. Start with a revenue bridge
  2. Separate variable and fixed costs
  3. Require quantified variance explanations
  4. Connect profit to cash
  5. Record owner decisions and follow-up

What to measure

  • GOP and margin
  • Cash flow
  • Forecast accuracy

Mistakes to avoid

  • Accepting narrative explanations
  • Confusing profit with cash
  • Hiding variance by rebasing the budget
