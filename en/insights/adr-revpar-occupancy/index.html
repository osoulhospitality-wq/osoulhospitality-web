ADR, RevPAR and Occupancy: What Does Each Metric Reveal?

Read occupancy, ADR and RevPAR together to see how rooms sold and room rates contributed to revenue.

By Y. A. — Osool Hospitality8 August 20261 min read

Worked example

If 80 of 100 available rooms sell for SAR 24,000 in room revenue, occupancy is 80%, ADR is SAR 300 and RevPAR is SAR 240. Confirm what is deducted from net revenue before comparing channels.

What to check

  • Definition of available and sold rooms
  • Period and comparison market
  • Included and excluded revenue
  • Rate-volume mix

Steps to take

  1. Lock the metric dictionary
  2. Analyse rate and occupancy together
  3. Use Net RevPAR for channel decisions
  4. Connect results to demand
  5. Document variance explanations

What to measure

  • ADR
  • Occupancy
  • RevPAR and Net RevPAR

Mistakes to avoid

  • Reading one metric in isolation
  • Changing the denominator
  • Comparing unlike properties
