Development & opening

Operating Concept and Unit Mix Before Design Freeze

Compare unit layouts against the guests you expect to serve, how long they stay and the cost of servicing each unit.

By Y. A. — Osool Hospitality8 August 20261 min read

Worked example

Before freezing the design, compare a two-bedroom apartment with two smaller units on expected rate, cleaning, circulation space and average stay length. Record the unit-mix decision before ordering equipment.

What to check

  • Demand segments and use cases
  • Required duration and capacity
  • Cost to serve each type
  • Future conversion flexibility

Steps to take

  1. Write the product hypothesis
  2. Test the mix financially
  3. Review sellable-area efficiency
  4. Model operations for every type
  5. Design deliberate flexibility

What to measure

  • Revenue per square metre
  • Unit-type margin
  • Space efficiency

Mistakes to avoid

  • Copying a neighbouring project
  • Creating too many types
  • Ignoring stores and service space
