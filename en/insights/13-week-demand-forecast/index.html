A 13-Week Demand Forecast Linking Sales, Pricing and Operations

Update the next 13 weeks of demand weekly to plan room availability, staffing, purchases and cash.

By Y. A. — Osoul HospitalityUpdated 27 November 20261 min read

Worked example

If pickup jumps in week six, check how many group bookings can still cancel. Update occupancy and housekeeping plans before increasing staff coverage.

What to check

  • On-books demand and pickup
  • Events and demand generators
  • Cancellations and expected booking reductions
  • Operating capacity

Steps to take

  1. Refresh the forecast weekly
  2. Separate segments and channels
  3. Record assumptions
  4. Attach an action to every variance
  5. Compare forecast with actual

What to measure

  • Forecast accuracy
  • New bookings versus the forecast requirement
  • Capacity gap

An evidence check for the owner

Match planned outages to forecast demand. Identify affected rooms, required shutdowns and temporary cover before confirming a contractor’s work window.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Copying the monthly budget
  • One number with no scenario
  • Changing assumptions without a record
