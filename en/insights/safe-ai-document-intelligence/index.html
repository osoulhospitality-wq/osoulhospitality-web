Safe, Useful Document Intelligence in Hospitality

Use AI to extract and compare information, then check the output against the original documents and restrict access to sensitive data.

By Y. A. — Osoul HospitalityUpdated 27 November 20261 min read

Worked example

A system may extract a response time from an old addendum. Show the reviewer the page number and contract version beside the extracted value and require a human to approve the field.

What to check

  • Document type and sensitivity
  • Use decision and boundary
  • Source of truth
  • Error and leakage risk

Steps to take

  1. Classify data before upload
  2. Use an appropriate environment and contract
  3. Require page and clause citations
  4. Human-review critical samples
  5. Log model, version and decision

What to measure

  • Extraction accuracy
  • Review time
  • Critical errors caught

An evidence check for the owner

When extracting a response commitment from a contract, retain its version, clause and page. Have the contract owner verify the wording before using it in a supplier dashboard.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Uploading a confidential contract to a public tool
  • Accepting a summary without source
  • Allowing the model to make the final decision
