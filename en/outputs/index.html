How owners measure facilities performance

Before renewing a contract, look at what was due, what passed inspection and what failed again. Agree the service scope, critical assets and measurement period with each provider.

Business continuity

Which service is at risk and how will guests be served during an outage?

Rank assets by the impact of failure. The outage record carries the loss-of-service time, verified restoration time and escalation owner. Open outages remain visible.

Supplier workmanship

Was the job accepted at its first inspection?

First-pass acceptance is the number of jobs accepted at first inspection divided by all jobs first inspected during the period. Show the sample size, reason for rejection and owner of rework.

Service-level performance

Did the provider respond and restore service within the contracted windows?

Measure response and restoration separately against the deadline for each priority. Use the orders due in the period and include open overdue orders in the denominator.

Technical efficiency

Did the repair address the cause of failure?

Match the asset and fault code within the agreed observation window. Report repairs followed by a repeat fault against repairs that reached the end of that window.

Administrative and financial waste

Why did a visit recur or an invoice come back for review?

Match the work order, acceptance record and invoice line. Identify repeat visits and disputed charges. Record recovered costs only after settlement is documented.

Sustainability and resource efficiency

Did consumption change under comparable conditions?

Compare meter readings against a baseline with occupancy, weather and meter coverage recorded. Distinguish gaps in readings from an actual reduction.

Compare providers only across similar work, priority and evidence coverage. Show the period and record source next to each measure. When evidence is missing, report the gap.
