How we take over and run a property

Before responsibilities transfer, we identify what can operate, what needs attention and who will resolve open items. Reception receives a reporting route for its first shift.

Request a service proposal

Mobilisation and handover

We walk the site with the operator, reconcile equipment to contracts and warranties and document the baseline with approved photographs. Both parties record exceptions, responsibility transfer dates and inherited work in the handover record.

Service levels and escalation

We distinguish personal danger, essential service loss, local defects and planned requests. The schedule sets separate targets for acknowledgement, attendance, restoration and permanent repair. Waiting for authority or parts is recorded and escalated under the agreed rule.

Shift and owner reporting

The operator receives outstanding items and their effect on available units. The owner receives spending and exceptions, recurring fault causes and decisions with options, estimates, owners and follow-up dates.
